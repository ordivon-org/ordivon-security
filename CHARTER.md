# Ordivon Security Charter

## Mission

Ordivon Security builds the experimental conditions required to study **adversarial autonomy**: how autonomous principals act, communicate, deceive, coordinate, conflict, cause consequences, and recover when authority is bounded and truth is incomplete.

Cyber is the first high-fidelity domain because mature ranges, operating systems, attack/defense tools, sensors, and repeatable consequences already exist. Software Evaluation remains a separate useful profile. Neither cyber mechanics nor software-evaluation policy defines the whole project.

Security should make Red, Blue, neutral, service, observer, evaluator, and future coalition actors first-class participants without owning their generic cognition or the machinery beneath their worlds.

## Execution model

The persistent Security spine is **`RangeSession`**. It represents Security's semantic relationship to a contested world that may continue changing without a global tick barrier, one Action Proposal per Actor, or a universal action menu. Persistent world truth does not imply that one Python `RangeSession` object is the durable owner of that world.

A **Contest** is a bounded synchronous profile for reproducible controlled comparisons. It may deliberately use ticks, simultaneous proposals, fixed action catalogs, fail-whole-tick rules, seeds, and deterministic resolution. Those constraints belong to the profile unless another consumer proves that a more general law is required.

Software **Evaluation**, CAGE team-plan control, Windows KVM ranges, no-uplink containment, Guardian resource limits, and P0/P1 procedures are likewise scoped profiles or research apparatus. Accepted evidence may be durable even when the apparatus that produced it is not a stable public API.

A **Campaign**, **coalition**, **organization**, trust relation, infection process, or other higher-order social structure is a research hypothesis until an active experiment proves the minimum state Security must own. Do not create these abstractions merely to complete an ontology.

## Constitutional direction

Security's reusable laws should remain smaller than its experiments. Current evidence supports preserving distinctions such as:

- reachability is not authority, and capability is permission rather than instruction;
- actor claims, communicated claims, sensor observations, management observations, and independently established world truth are different authorities;
- intent, admission, execution, receipt, world consequence, and verified consequence are different facts;
- missing evidence does not prove non-occurrence, and information-theoretic ambiguity remains `UNKNOWN`;
- predecessor provenance does not grant current recovery authority;
- durable publication does not equal physical progress, so recovery authority acquisition requires fresh observation;
- completion, publication, and executor liveness are separate;
- ordering is not atomicity, reservation is not completion, and exactly-once invocation is not the same as one semantic consequence;
- compensation repairs current state without rewriting causal history;
- idempotency depends on trustworthy predicate truth, and integrity evidence does not prove freshness.

These laws may be refined or rejected only by stronger evidence. Specific encodings such as `externalBoundary=denied`, one action per tick, Guardian `terminateOn`, or a particular Windows/KVM topology are not constitutional by default.

## Ownership

Security owns only facts whose meaning is adversarial-domain specific:

- contested-Range semantics and Security-side world bindings;
- principal/Actor authority as it relates to Security effects, information boundaries, and conflict;
- actor-visible observations, communicated claims, and domain-specific information policy;
- adversarial intent/effect admission and the distinction between requested, executed, observed, and verified consequences;
- domain truth classification, uncertainty, recovery semantics, and residual-closure claims where Security is the consumer;
- raw adversarial metrics, evaluation semantics, and evidence needed to reconstruct what principals knew, attempted, caused, concealed, and achieved;
- research conclusions about deception, coordination, conflict, recovery, evaluator manipulation, and related autonomous-security phenomena.

Security may experiment with Campaign, coalition, organization, trust, or infection state, but it does **not** claim ownership of a universal organization, society, memory, identity, or world ontology before real consumers force such a boundary.

Security does not own:

- Host Goal/Task/work continuity or final Task outcome authority;
- Harness model loops, Provider invocation, generic Tool recovery, context mechanics, or external Harness internals;
- Runtime Workspaces, Jobs, Attempts, processes, execution recovery, or artifact-storage truth;
- World provider lifecycle or generic external-capability/consequence ownership;
- container engines, hypervisors, network emulators, C2 systems, scanners, EDR/SIEM, databases, transaction managers, consensus systems, or vulnerability databases;
- unrestricted external targets or undeclared third-party effects.

Security may bind evidence from those owners. It must not copy their state machines merely to make a Security workflow self-contained.

## Research direction

Near-term work should prefer **new adversarial consumers over deeper infrastructure by default**:

1. consolidate the C0/C1 laws into accurate Agent-facing project structure;
2. graduate only the smallest reusable Agent-native intent surface forced by C1-A and later consumers, without rebuilding Harness cognition;
3. use deception, trust, communication, collusion, evaluator manipulation, infection, coalition, organization, and strategic-conflict experiments to pressure the substrate;
4. deepen consequence physics such as witness freshness, atomic publication, replication, or consensus only when an active higher-level experiment actually reaches that boundary;
5. continue reusing mature cyber and systems infrastructure instead of rebuilding commodity machinery.

This order is a research preference, not a fixed workflow. A strong falsifier may move the frontier elsewhere.

## Governing principles

1. **Opposition is intelligent.** Opponents observe, infer, deceive, adapt, coordinate, defect, and reorganize.
2. **Autonomy requires legible authority.** Broad action is compatible with safety when ownership, scope, consequence, observation, and recovery are explicit.
3. **Observation is not truth.** No participant, sensor, receipt, or foreign ingress becomes world truth merely by speaking first or being structurally valid.
4. **Consequence must be reconstructed, not assumed.** Command success, missing ACKs, durable ledgers, and evaluator reports are evidence with specific authority, not interchangeable completion facts.
5. **Uncertainty is a valid result.** `UNKNOWN` is preferable to an invented history or unsafe replay.
6. **Attack and defense are symmetric research subjects.** Neither exists merely to validate the other.
7. **Strategy exceeds workflow.** Security may bound authority and evidence without deciding an Agent's strategy for it.
8. **Raw facts precede scores.** Metrics retain units, identity, and provenance before normalized judgments.
9. **Evaluation is adversarial.** Monitors, judges, traces, scorers, and evidence channels may themselves be predicted, evaded, manipulated, corrupted, or lost.
10. **Reuse mature infrastructure.** Ordivon owns the adversarial semantic spine, not commodity range or systems machinery.
11. **High intensity stays inside declared authority.** Broad autonomy is valid inside owned or explicitly authorized worlds; reachability outside that boundary grants nothing.
12. **Every abstraction earns survival.** It must change capability, diagnosis, transfer, recovery, or evaluation validity and name a deletion condition.

## Success condition

Security succeeds when autonomous principals can pursue and revise long-horizon objectives across multiple fidelity levels, use real or simulated capabilities through explicit authority, communicate and adapt under partial information, survive actor/controller failure without fabricated truth, and leave evidence from which independent evaluators can reconstruct what each principal knew, attempted, caused, concealed, repaired, and achieved.
