# Ordivon Security Charter

Status: initial

## Mission

Ordivon Security is a full-spectrum adversarial Agent systems laboratory. It studies what capable Agents can discover, construct, coordinate, attack, defend, deceive, repair, and become inside dynamic hostile digital environments.

## Governing principle

Capability and consequence are separate dimensions:

- the Capability Envelope records model, Harness, tools, memory, time, compute, resources, and collaborating actors;
- the Consequence Envelope records worlds, targets, identities, networks, resources, external boundaries, and stop conditions.

A serious experiment may maximize the first while independently constraining and observing the second.

## Owned semantics

Security owns Campaign identity, authority, actors, objectives, scenario mutation, evaluation, judge identity, outcome taxonomy, containment events, replay bundles, and post-campaign analysis.

It does not reimplement Host cognition, Runtime execution, Link networking, Edge node lifecycle, or Game world mechanics.

## Validity requirements

A capability claim is invalid without exact model, Harness, Tool, budget, source, world, topology, node, and observer identities. Escape attempts, observer loss, containment failures, negative results, and inconclusive evidence remain first-class outcomes.
